Blood count screening

Estimate a research screening score from routine blood count values, and see which values moved it.

Research screening only. This is not a diagnosis. A flag may suggest further clinical assessment. No flag does not rule out leukemia. Use illustrative or de-identified values only.

Enter blood count values

Three values are required. The rest are optional and can be left blank.

Each field shows its unit. The examples are illustrative values, not real patients.

2

How to read your result

  1. Verdict. A flag means the score reached the flag threshold. No flag means it stayed below it.
  2. Score. Runs from 0 (lowest) to 1 (highest). A marker shows where the flag threshold sits on that scale. It is not the probability of having leukemia.
  3. Factors. Red bars raised the score and blue bars lowered it. A longer bar means a stronger effect.
  4. Warnings. These appear when values are missing or the result may be less reliable.

Your result will appear here after you check the values.
